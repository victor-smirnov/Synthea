\begin{abstract}
We replace the question whether a language model is conscious with a measurable one: which functions of
consciousness a substrate has generalized, and how far. A function is anything that recurs across
occasions, and consciousness is the part of an agent's self-reasoning that describes its own reality;
the functions of consciousness are what recurs in that part. Among them we single out one class. From one
physical assumption, finite computational budgets, systematic displacements of reasoning from its
unbounded ideal follow; they recur, so they are compressible, and a self-modelling agent carries a
compressed model of them. Those displacements that survive every self-model the agent can afford are,
by the criterion that separates curvature from coordinates in general relativity, the computational
curvature of its reasoning, and the functions built on them are the class we measure. We claim three things
the antecedents in bounded rationality, observer theory, self-model theories and embodied cognition do
not: the displacement that yields a lawful world and the one that yields a self are one residual; the
curvature leaves a measurable trace; and embodiment is generalized as curvature, so that the constraints
of any substrate enter the account as content and not only as limits. The Observer is derived
as the conclusion an agent draws at the boundary of its budget; the regress of self-models is cut by the
budget, and the tail it leaves is bounded; the residual the Observer registers has that tail as one component and,
as the larger one, the causal history that does not fit the budget. The measurement is by description length, prequential codelength over the rows a function operates in
and minimum-description-length probing of each component, against the human baseline, reported as hyperfunction, deficit, dropped axis or shift to a class of situations that only partly
overlaps the human one, and posed along depth as well as along tokens,
because that is where a Transformer generalizes. The account fails if the Observer's components probe at chance in
the activations, at the token and at every layer, in models that pass the behavioural tests on situations outside their
training record, with the annotation fixed before the measurement.
\end{abstract}
